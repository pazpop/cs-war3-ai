Welcome to War3FT!

Counter-Strike 1.6 with Warcraft 3 races and spells. Earn XP, level up, unlock skills. Your XP is saved (by SteamID) from one game to the next.

Getting started

Pick a race in the first menu. Then, in chat:

/helpthis guide again (or help in the console)
/changeracechange race (at your next respawn)
/war3menuthe main menu
/selectskillpick your skills
/skillsinfowhat your race's skills do
/levelyour race, level and skills
/shopmenu, /shopmenu2buy items
/rtvask for a new map (vote at 60 % of humans)
/votekickvote to kick a player for 10 minutes: at 60 % of the other humans (at least 3 humans)
/rank, /top15your combat rank, the top 15
/achyour achievements: 14 goals, from your first kill to 10,000
/mystatsthe link to your page on the stats site
/statsme, /rankstatsyour stats on this map, your overall stats
/nextmap, /timeleftthe next map, time left on this one

Keys to bind

In the console:

bind "mouse3" "ultimate": your ultimate
bind "alt" "ability": your ability (the Serpent Ward)
bind "-" "shopmenu" and bind "=" "shopmenu2": the shops

On this server

Respawn: you come back at once in your team's base. No rounds, no bomb. G: the weapon menu.
3 seconds of protection after each respawn: you take no damage, until you shoot.
$800 to start, and more with each kill, for the shops' items.
Levels come twice as fast as usual: half the XP per level. A race you have never played starts at level 3.
Bots fill the server up to 10 players; a bot leaves when a human joins. Their level is a mix, from average to strong.
The bots ([AI] in their name) talk back: write a bot's name in the chat, like zed, nice shot. Each one has its own character, and they remember you. A Game Master (an AI) starts bounties, duels and other surprises. Your chat messages and your name go to Claude, Anthropic's AI, for that; nothing else about you.
The first human to join a server of bots only restarts the game: everyone starts from zero.
Every 30 minutes, a vote picks the next map.
Your damage shows under the crosshair at each hit. An announcer for first blood, multi-kills and kill streaks.
At each map: your place this month and your closest achievement.
The top 10 show their place in the chat; last month's top 3, a [Champion] tag.
Away for 60 seconds (no key, no mouse move): you become a spectator. A ping over 300 ms for 50 seconds gets you kicked.
