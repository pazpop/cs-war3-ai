Welcome to War3FT!

Counter-Strike 1.6 with Warcraft 3 races and spells. Earn XP, level up, unlock skills. Your XP is saved (by SteamID) from one game to the next.

Rules

  • 13 or older, as on Steam.
  • Respect: trash talk about the game is fine. Insults, threats and harassment are not.
  • No hate: no racism, sexism, homophobia or slurs, in any language, in the chat or in your name.
  • Nothing sexual or illegal, and no personal information, yours or anyone else's.
  • No cheating: no hacks, scripts, or bugs used on purpose.
  • No spam, and no ads for other servers. Changing your name 10 times within 10 minutes gets you kicked for an hour.
  • Away for 60 seconds (no key, no mouse move): you become a spectator. A ping over 300 ms for 50 seconds gets you kicked.

The same rules hold in the team chat. Break them, and the players can kick you for 10 minutes (/votekick), or an admin can kick or ban you.

Getting started

Pick a race in the first menu. Then, in chat:

/helpthis guide again (or help in the console)
/changeracechange race (at your next respawn)
/war3menuthe main menu
/selectskillpick your skills
/skillsinfowhat your race's skills do
/levelyour race, level and skills
/shopmenu, /shopmenu2buy items
/rtvask for a new map (vote at 60 % of humans)
/votekickvote to kick a player for 10 minutes: at 60 % of the other humans (at least 3 humans)
/rank, /top15your combat rank, the top 15
/achyour achievements: 14 goals, from your first kill to 10,000
/mystatsthe link to your page on the stats site
/coacha tip from the AI coach, from your numbers on this map, in private; or ask it a question: /coach what is my best weapon? /coach on: a tip every 5 minutes.
/goalthe goal of the week, the same for everyone, and your count
/statsme, /rankstatsyour stats on this map, your overall stats
/nextmap, /timeleftthe next map, time left on this one

Keys to bind

In the console:

bind "mouse3" "ultimate": your ultimate
bind "alt" "ability": your ability (the Serpent Ward)
bind "-" "shopmenu" and bind "=" "shopmenu2": the shops
bind "F3" "war3menu": the War3FT menu (race, skills, shops, help)

On this server

The game

  • Respawn: you come back at once in your team's base. No rounds, no bomb.
  • G: the weapon menu.
  • 3 seconds of protection after each respawn, until you shoot.
  • $800 to start, more with each kill, for the shops.
  • Every 20 minutes, a vote picks the next map.

Your progress

  • Levels come twice as fast. A race you have never played starts at level 3.
  • Your damage shows under the crosshair. An announcer for first blood and kill streaks.
  • At each map: the month's top 5 and your place. Your achievements: /ach.
  • The goal of the week, the same for everyone, a new one every Monday: /goal, and its board on the stats site.
  • /coach: a tip from the AI coach, in private. /coach on: one every 5 minutes (/coach off to stop). Near each map's end, you get a summary.
  • The top 10 show their place in the chat; last month's top 3 a [Champion] tag.

The bots

  • They fill the server up to 10 players; one leaves when a human joins.
  • They talk back ([AI] in their name): write a bot's name in the chat, like zed, nice shot. Each one has its own character and War3FT race, and they remember you, and the last map you played together: kill one often, and it holds a grudge. The great rivalries are on the bots' page.
  • Bots are level 1 to 5, new at each map: your XP is yours, theirs never piles up.
  • Ask them anything about the game: a message with a ? usually gets an answer, like which race is good for a beginner?
  • Join a server of bots only, and the game restarts: every score of the map starts from zero (XP and rankings stay).

The Game Master

An AI watches the score. Halfway through each map, it may start one of these, announced in the chat and on your screen. Nothing is forced on you: play as you like, the prizes are a bonus.

BountyA player far ahead gets a price on their head: the prize to whoever kills them, for 3 minutes.
DuelTwo players of opposite teams: the first to kill the other wins the prize, within 3 minutes.
ChallengeA goal for every human, within 2 minutes: kills, headshots or knife kills. The first to reach it wins the prize. Your count shows in the chat.
BossA bot glows red, with 500 health: whoever takes it down wins the prize. After 3 minutes, it is a normal bot again.
Low gravity30 seconds of huge jumps for everyone.
Gold rain$1500 for every player.
XP rain100 XP for every human, for the race you play.
GiftA War3FT shop item for everyone: boots, claws, a cloak, a ring...

The prize: $3000 and 200 XP, saved with your race like the rest of your XP. Each night, the Game Master writes the server's journal on the stats site. At each map's end, a [STATS] line names the map's MVP (counted on the stats site when two humans or more scored).

Public on this site, for as long as the server runs: your name, XP, skills, stats, achievements (with their date) and rankings, kept under your SteamID (that is how your progress is saved); your rivalries with the bots, until their memory of you goes; a bot's line (with its time, 30 days at most) or the journal may name you too. To have your name taken off: ask an admin in the game, or open an issue at github.com/pazpop/cs-war3-ai. The server's logs (with your IP) go after 14 days, its chat log (team chat and the coach's tips included) after 3 months. The bots, the coach and the Game Master are an AI: Claude, by Anthropic, which keeps what it gets under its own policy. Sent to it: your public chat messages, your name, your score and how you fare against the bots (since when and when last you played here, your last map, your kills and deaths against them), and for the coach your numbers on the map and your question. Never your SteamID or your IP. The bots' memory of you stays on this server and goes after 6 months without a visit (backups keep it a little longer). Before a line is shown, a second AI check holds back what it finds offensive; it checks names too, and an offensive one becomes "Player".
